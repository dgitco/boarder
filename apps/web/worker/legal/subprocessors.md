# Sub-processors

_Last updated: October 29, 2026_

Boarder uses these companies to run the service. Changes are announced here and by email to workspace owners at least 30 days ahead, as the [Data Processing Agreement](/policy/dpa) describes. We, {{OPERATOR}} (ldg@dgit.co), run the service from the Republic of Korea.

| Company | What it does for Boarder | Where | Transfer safeguard |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cloudflare, Inc. | Hosting (Workers, D1, R2, Durable Objects), text extraction from Drive files (Workers AI, part of Cloudflare's platform), receiving mail sent to Drive folders (Email Routing), email delivery, page-view counts | Global network; data primarily in the United States and Asia-Pacific | EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework |
| PostHog, Inc. | Product analytics (pages viewed, with cookies only after consent; tool names and outcomes; no tool arguments or results) | United States (PostHog US Cloud) | EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework |
| Polar Software, Inc. | Billing as merchant of record (payments by Stripe), for paid plans | United States | Standard Contractual Clauses |

Services your members connect (Slack, Linear, Google and so on) aren't sub-processors: your members choose them and sign in with their own accounts, and those services act under their own terms. Google and GitHub are used only when someone chooses to sign in with them. ntfy.sh (or another ntfy server) receives notifications only if a member sets an ntfy topic for them: that's the member's choice, not a sub-processor of ours.
